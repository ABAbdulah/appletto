What we do

One team, the whole product

We are not a reseller or an agency that hands work off. Design, engineering and release management all sit under one roof.

01

Android development

Native Kotlin and cross-platform builds, published and maintained under our own Google Play developer account.

02

iOS development

Swift and cross-platform apps built to pass App Store review the first time and feel native on every device.

03

Product & UI design

Interface design, flows and store assets — the part that decides whether anyone keeps the app after day one.

04

Backend & APIs

The services behind the app: authentication, payments, notifications, analytics and the infrastructure to run them.

The company

A registered software house, not a side project

APPLETTO (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED is an incorporated software company. We build our own apps and take on client products, and we stay on them after release — updates, store compliance, crash fixes and new features.

If you need a partner who can take an idea to a live listing on Google Play and keep it healthy, that is the job we do every day.
